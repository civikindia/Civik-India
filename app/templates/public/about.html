About Civik India

A youth-led public awareness and civic accountability platform.

Civik India is a youth-led public awareness platform helping citizens raise their voice against corruption, bribery, and public service issues without creating an account or revealing their identity.

Civik India is an independent public awareness initiative and is not affiliated with any government department, political party, or law enforcement authority.

Follow Civik India on Instagram: @civik.india

Anonymous by Design

Citizens can submit complaints without login or registration.

Transparent Tracking

Each complaint receives a tracking ID for status visibility.

Independent Awareness

Built to improve public awareness, civic participation, and accountability.

Core Objectives

  • Reduce barriers to reporting misconduct.
  • Improve redressal speed across departments.
  • Provide measurable public transparency.
